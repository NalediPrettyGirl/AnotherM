Our Story

Your outfit deserves more than one moment.
Welcome to ANOTHER MOMENT.

Why We Started

Finding the perfect dress or suit for a special occasion can be a stressful and expensive experience. Often, these beautiful garments are worn only once, before being packed away in a closet and forgotten.

At ANOTHER MOMENT, we believe every exquisite piece of attire deserves more than just one day in the spotlight. Our platform connects people looking for high-quality, pre-loved event wear—from stunning wedding dresses to elegant matric dance gowns—with individuals ready to give their cherished garments a second life.

Sustainability

By choosing pre-loved formal wear, you are actively participating in sustainable fashion. Not only do you save money and find unique pieces, but you also help reduce the environmental impact of the clothing industry. Look beautiful while being kind to the planet.

Our Mission

Whether you are a bride on a budget, a mother looking for a handsome suit for her son, or someone who wants to make some extra cash by selling or renting out their wardrobe, our marketplace is built for you.
